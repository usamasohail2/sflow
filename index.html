How it works

Steps

  1. Upload an SVG of your signature, or use Trace a photo to create one from an image.
  2. Adjust Animation (speed, easing, loop), Stroke, and Paths in the panel.
  3. Export as Animated SVG, MP4, or GIF.

Best practices

  • Use a single-path or clean multi-path SVG for the smoothest animation.
  • Keep stroke width consistent; use the stroke scale control to fine-tune.
  • For photo trace, use a high-contrast image on a plain background.
  • Reorder paths in the panel to change draw order if needed.
